# Publishing CPR Beat to the stores

Everything the stores ask for, and where it lives in this repo. Facts verified in October
2026; re-check the consoles, they change often.

From 2026-10-09 the targets are **RuStore, F-Droid, AppGallery, IzzyOnDroid and GitHub
Releases**. Google Play and Galaxy Store are closed to this personal account: both need an
organization (sections at the end). Per-store research: SPEC.md section 11.6.

## RuStore (rustore.ru)

1. Developer account at console.rustore.ru: type "Физическое лицо", sign in with VK ID. Free and
   instant; no ID check unless monetization is switched on, but the agreement (§12.3) lets
   RuStore ask for documents later. Non-residents choose their country and sign the
   non-resident agreement.
2. New app → upload the signed **APK** (AAB also accepted).
3. Russian card from `store/listings/rustore.md` (single language, FAQ block optional, no AHA/ERC
   names), category **Здоровье** with no second category, screenshots from `store/screenshots/9x16/ru-RU/`
   (RuStore crops to 9:16; tablet screenshots optional, phone shots are shown on tablets), icon, age
   rating 0+, «Безопасность данных» section (no data), e-mail contact. Countries: «Страны и
   регионы» in the console. Privacy policy URL only if data is processed.
4. No health declaration. Review within 72 hours; moderators may ask for more data on the first
   publication and may move the category. Appeals to support@rustore.ru.

## F-Droid

1. Free account on gitlab.com (F-Droid has no accounts of its own).
2. Fork gitlab.com/fdroid/fdroiddata, add `metadata/dev.alcini.cprbeat.yml` from
   `store/fdroid/dev.alcini.cprbeat.yml`, open a merge request. Checklist: the tag `v0.3.0` must
   exist on GitHub; F-Droid builds `assembleRelease` from source without our keystore.
3. Review queue is long (about 900 open merge requests in October 2026); after the merge the app
   appears within 24–48 hours. Categories Sports & Health and Timer. No medical rules, no identity check, no fee.
4. Signing: the build is reproducible, so F-Droid publishes our own signature (`Binaries` +
   `AllowedAPKSigningKeys`) and its APK updates a RuStore/GitHub install and vice versa. F-Droid
   builds the tag, downloads `cpr-beat-X.Y.Z.apk` from the GitHub release and publishes only if
   the two match apart from the signature. So every release: build the APK from the tagged
   commit and attach it under that exact name before F-Droid's updater picks up the tag;
   otherwise that version fails there until fixed. Checked locally for 0.3.0 on 2026-10-09
   (`fdroid lint`, `rewritemeta`, `fdroid build -l` with fdroidserver 2.4.5, Gradle 9.7.1;
   identical on JDK 25 and on a clean JDK 17 build without the Gradle cache).
5. Submitted 2026-10-10: https://gitlab.com/fdroid/fdroiddata/-/merge_requests/51877 from the
   fork gitlab.com/alessio.alcini.it/fdroiddata. CI does not run for unverified new GitLab
   accounts; per the MR template we asked the maintainers to trigger it instead of adding a phone
   number or card.

## Huawei AppGallery

1. AppGallery Connect account (developer.huawei.com), individual identity verification 1–2 days:
   ID or passport with the name in Latin letters plus a bank document. Account exists.
2. My apps → New app → Android → upload the signed **APK** (an AAB needs AppGallery App Signing
   first); no HMS Core needed.
3. App information from `store/listings/appgallery.md`: default English (US), Russian under
   Localization; screenshots from `store/screenshots/9x16/`; category Sports & health → Health; content rating 3+; privacy policy URL
   (validated); copyright/qualification not required outside China. Exclude mainland China.
4. Reviewer notes: offline metronome and timer, provides no medical or healthcare service, not a
   medical device, collects no data (guideline 11.4 reserves healthcare services for legal
   entities). Guideline 1.2 may object to "CPR" in the name.
5. Review 1–5 working days; they check that the app runs on phones without Google services — we
   use none.

## IzzyOnDroid

1. Free account on codeberg.org; request via the "App Inclusion Request" template at
   codeberg.org/IzzyOnDroid/repodata.
2. Needs a published GitHub release with the signed APK attached (draft releases are invisible),
   tag matching versionName; fastlane metadata in the repo.
3. The policy rejects LLM-generated code ("Vibe-coded apps will be rejected"). State the AI
   assistance honestly: the commits carry Claude co-author trailers. Expect a decline.
4. Categories: Sports & Health, Time.

## GitHub Releases

1. `./gradlew assembleRelease` with `keystore.properties`, then attach
   `app/build/outputs/apk/release/app-release.apk` (renamed `cpr-beat-<version>.apk`) and its
   SHA-256 to the release `v<version>`. Release notes from `CHANGELOG.md`.
2. Same signature as RuStore and AppGallery, so users can move between them without reinstalling.
   Copies installed from the Play closed test are signed by Google (Play App Signing) and must be
   uninstalled first: `INSTALL_FAILED_UPDATE_INCOMPATIBLE` otherwise.

## Closed: Google Play

Closed for this personal account since 2026-10-09. The second submission (category Tools, Medical
and Health & Fitness tags removed, declaration "Emergency and First Aid") was rejected with the
same reason, so the declaration triggers the organization rule. Play Console's support assistant
confirmed it: every Medical declaration needs an organization account, a CPR training framing
included; only an app without any CPR feature or wording may declare "no health features". The
owner will not create a legal entity. Steps and history below are kept for reference.

1. Play Console account: US$25 once, government ID and payment profile. Personal accounts show
   the legal name, country and e-mail publicly.
2. Create the app → upload the **AAB** → accept Play App Signing (Google re-signs with its own key;
   our key becomes the upload key).
3. App content: privacy policy URL, Data safety ("no data collected or shared"), **Health apps
   declaration** → feature "Emergency and First Aid" (not "Medical Device Apps"), ads "no",
   content rating questionnaire, target audience "18+ or everyone". Category **Tools**, not
   Medical (see "Rejected on 2026-10-09" below).
4. Store listing: texts from `store/listings/google-play.md` (en-US default, ru-RU via Manage
   translations), icon, feature graphic (required), at least 2 phone screenshots. Tick Russia in
   the production countries list.
5. Personal account created after 2023-11-13: **closed test with ≥ 12 testers opted in for 14
   consecutive days**, then "apply for production access". Friends with the APK can be those
   testers: add their Google e-mails to the closed-test list.
6. Target API 36 is required for new apps since 2026-08-31; we target 36.

### Rejected on 2026-10-09: health apps need an organization account

The first submission (category Medical, Health apps declaration "Emergency and First Aid") was
rejected: "Violation of Play Console Requirements. Some types of apps can only be distributed by
organizations. You have selected an app category or declared your app offers certain features
that require you to submit your app using an organization account." Since 2024-08-31 new
developer accounts must register as an organization for "Health apps, such as Medical apps and
Human Subjects Research apps" (Play Console Requirements, answer 10788890). Our account is
personal and was created in 2026.

Google does not say whether the category, the declaration or both triggered it, and no Help
Center page maps declaration items or categories to the organization rule. "Emergency and First
Aid" sits in the Medical group of the declaration form (answer 14738291). The appeal link in the
e-mail went in circles, so no appeal was filed.

Second attempt: category **Tools**, declaration unchanged ("Emergency and First Aid" is the
accurate one for a CPR pacer), one resubmission from Publishing overview → Send for review.

- **Approved**: the category was the trigger; keep Tools.
- **Rejected again**: the declaration is the trigger. Do **not** switch to "My app doesn't provide
  any health features": the app's only purpose is CPR, Google reviews declarations for accuracy
  (Requirements 3.1, Misrepresentation policy) and repeated rejections can suspend the account.
  What is left is an organization account (legal entity, D-U-N-S number, organization website
  and e-mail; answers 13628312, 16260648) or leaving Play to the other stores and F-Droid.

Result: rejected again the same day. Play left; see the top of this section.

## Closed: Samsung Galaxy Store

Android apps need a corporate Commercial Seller with a D-U-N-S number, even free ones (Seller
Portal guide and console notice, checked 2026-10-09). Kept for reference:

1. Seller Portal account (seller.samsungapps.com). **Commercial Seller status is required even for
   free apps**: business address, government ID, bank account or PayPal, D-U-N-S number or
   equivalent documents in English, up to 10 business days each. Seller address, phone and e-mail
   are shown publicly; a gmail address needs an explanation in the application.
2. Add new app → binary: **APK**, signed; select "All devices" or Galaxy phones.
3. Listing in Advanced mode from `store/listings/galaxy-store.md`: English default, Russian as an
   added language, limits in bytes. Icon 512, 4–8 screenshots (the A52 shots qualify; one 16:9
   image makes the app eligible for promotion), category Health, age All, privacy policy URL.
4. Pre-review about 1 business day, then device test 2–4 business days during which the app can be
   suspended; the first launch must not crash.
